Different jobs. If the job is writing — emails, notes, prompts — Wispr Flow is the polished pro (you pay monthly for it). If the job is doing — send this, call her, book the airport, show my boarding pass, don't touch my money — RobotHand is the one that acts, with confirm-first laws and truthful evidence, free and private by default.

For VDX: we just borrowed Flow's look (the glassy compact bubble). The right product move is Flow-style dictation inside the VDX bubble for the words + RobotHand under the same bubble for the actions — that is the combination no rival currently ships.
